## What they cover for the Agent

- Device-code / account sign-in (`labwired agent login`)
- Hosted model gateway (`api.labwired.com/v1`) and remote MCP (`api.labwired.com/mcp`)
- Content you send (prompts, firmware, diagrams) for tools and models
- Local-only data on your machine (`~/.config/labwired-agent`, `~/.labwired/session`)
- No sale of personal data; no training foundation models on your firmware/prompts (see Privacy Policy)
- Simulation ≠ silicon; verify on real hardware for high-risk systems (see Terms)
